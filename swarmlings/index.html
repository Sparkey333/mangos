How to play

• Move: WASD / arrows, or drag on screen.
• Throw a creature: click / tap toward a target. It grabs cargo, fights foes, or breaks walls.
• Whistle (recall): Space, or the ⟳ button — pulls nearby creatures back to you.
• Grow: carry glowing cargo to your Hive to hatch more creatures.
• Pick type: bottom-left buttons. Each color resists a different hazard.
• Beat the day: bank cargo before the bar runs out. Strays left in the field are lost at dusk.
