# SWARMLINGS 🌱

A swarm-command prototype — direct a horde of little helpers to carry, fight,
and build against a day-timer. **Original IP**, one self-contained HTML5 file,
runs everywhere.

## Play

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any modern browser.

- **Move:** WASD / arrows, or drag on screen
- **Throw a creature:** click / tap toward a target (it grabs cargo, fights
  foes, or breaks hazard-walls)
- **Whistle (recall):** Space / right-click / the ⟳ button
- **Grow:** carry glowing cargo to your **Hive** to hatch more creatures
- **Pick type:** bottom-left buttons — each color resists a different hazard
- **Beat the day:** bank cargo before the bar empties; strays are lost at dusk

## The big idea

One engine, two complete reskins you can flip live (**⚙ Dials → World Skin**):

- **v2 · Bloomlings** — faithful-feeling, every IP-risky element swapped & logged
- **v3 · Tidewardens** — same engine, spirit kept, world changed entirely

Plus working **dials**: swarm cap, creature scale, leader speed, day length,
a **music amp** (volume + intensity), and a **fidelity** switch (Lite →
Hi-Def) for "go up from there when more design and hi-def are requested."
