# The Leave-Behind One-Pager (fill-in template)

This is what you send *that night* after a good conversation, or hand over when
someone's a real lead. One page. Skimmable in 20 seconds, complete in 90.

Make one per project. Keep them as PDFs in your phone (or a single link / QR that
lets them pick which project). The discipline of fitting it on one page is the
discipline that makes your thinking clear.

---

## The template (copy, fill, delete the brackets)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

  [PROJECT NAME]
  [One-line logline — the 10-sec line from on-the-spot-pitch.md]

  ── THE PROBLEM ──────────────────────────────────────────
  [Who] currently has to [painful status quo].
  It costs them [time / money / stress — be specific].

  ── WHAT WE DO ───────────────────────────────────────────
  [The new way, in 1–2 sentences. The shift, not the spec.]

  ── WHY IT MATTERS / WHY NOW ─────────────────────────────
  [The wedge nobody else nails + why this moment is right.]

  ── TRACTION (proof) ─────────────────────────────────────
  • [Number / user / pilot / result #1]
  • [Number / user / pilot / result #2]
  • Current stage: [honest stage]

  ── WHY ME ───────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Your unfair advantage in one line — lived it, built it,
   obsessed with it.]

  ── THE ASK ──────────────────────────────────────────────
  [The ONE specific thing you want from the reader:
   an intro to ___ / a pilot / feedback / a meeting.]

  ── CONTACT ──────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Name]  ·  [phone]  ·  [email]  ·  [one clean link / QR]

└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## Rules that keep it strong

- **One page. Always.** If it doesn't fit, your thinking isn't sharp enough yet —
  cut, don't shrink the font.
- **One specific ask**, not a menu. "Could you intro me to one contractor who'd
  pilot this?" beats "let me know if you can help!"
- **Numbers > adjectives.** "12 crews piloting" beats "lots of interest."
- **Lead with the problem, not you.** They care about the pain first, your bio last.
- **Make it match the card** it came from — same one or two fonts, same color, same
  restraint. The kit should feel like one hand made all of it.
- **Send it when you said you would.** The one-pager that arrives *that night* while
  the conversation is warm beats the perfect one that arrives next week.

---

## A "pick-your-project" landing idea (for the QR)

If you've got several ideas and don't know which will land with a given person,
point your card's QR at a tiny page that just says:

> **"Which one are you curious about?"** → [Project A] · [Project B] · [Project C]

…each linking to its one-pager (or a 20-second video). Now one card serves every
project, the visitor self-selects, and you learn which ideas pull — for free.
