# The Pitch Kit

> A system for sounding smooth, practiced, and professional the moment one of your
> ideas comes up in the wild — and for deciding how you show up on paper, in metal,
> in paper-cranes, and as a company.

You don't rise to the level of your ideas; you fall to the level of your systems.
This kit is the system. Build it once, draw from it forever.

## What's in here

| File | What it gives you | When to reach for it |
|---|---|---|
| `on-the-spot-pitch.md` | The verbal kit — 10-sec / 30-sec / 2-min versions, a fill-in template, objection comebacks | You're talking to a real human *right now* |
| `business-card-options.md` | The Red / Blue / Z decision on what you physically hand over (cards → cranes → 3D) | You're deciding what to print/make/order |
| `company-structure.md` | The Red / Blue / Z decision on umbrella company vs. many | You're deciding how it's all organized |
| `leave-behind-one-pager.md` | A fill-in one-pager template for any single project | You want something to send or hand over after |

## How to use it (the 3-minute version)

1. **Fill `on-the-spot-pitch.md` once per project.** Memorize only the 10-second
   line. Everything longer, you improvise *from* it.
2. **Pick a card tier from `business-card-options.md`.** Start at Z (the movable
   middle). You can always escalate to crane/3D for people who earn it.
3. **Decide structure later, not never.** Skim `company-structure.md` now, act on
   it when money or paperwork actually shows up — not before.

## The one rule that makes all of it work

Every page in here optimizes for the *next* small action, not the close. You're
not trying to sell on a sidewalk. You're trying to earn 30 more seconds, a saved
contact, a "send me that." Lower the bar, win more often.
