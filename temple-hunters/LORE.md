# LORE — The Unified Mapping

The game's premise: a single ladder runs through **color, light frequency,
sound, stone, sacred geometry, the body's energy centers, and the physical
layers of a great pyramid.** Each of the seven Temple Hunters *is* one rung.
This file is the master table the game is generated from, plus honest notes on
what's grounded vs. what's symbolic so you can keep building the real theory
underneath the fiction.

> The game reads its numbers from `js/data.js`. If you change the theory, change
> it there and the whole app follows.

## The seven rungs

### 1 · RED — PYROKK · Root
- **Light:** red, ~430 THz (longest visible wavelength, ~700 nm)
- **Sound:** 396 Hz (solfeggio — "liberating fear / grounding")
- **Chakra:** Muladhara (root) — survival, foundation
- **Stone/Gem:** Garnet, bloodstone, red jasper
- **Geometry:** Cube → classical **Earth**
- **Pyramid layer:** **Aswan red granite**, quarried ~900 km south and floated
  down the Nile for the King's Chamber and its relieving stones. *(Grounded.)*

### 2 · ORANGE — EMBYRX · Sacral
- **Light:** orange, ~490 THz
- **Sound:** 417 Hz ("undoing situations / facilitating change")
- **Chakra:** Svadhisthana (sacral) — creativity, flow, appetite
- **Stone/Gem:** Carnelian, amber, sunstone
- **Geometry:** Tetrahedron → classical **Fire**
- **Pyramid layer:** **Basalt pavement** at Giza, quarried at Widan el-Faras in
  the Fayum and hauled overland + by water. *(Grounded — basalt pavement is
  real; the color tie is symbolic.)*

### 3 · YELLOW — SOLARA · Solar Plexus
- **Light:** yellow, ~520 THz
- **Sound:** 528 Hz (the "miracle"/"repair" tone in solfeggio lore)
- **Chakra:** Manipura (solar plexus) — will, drive, identity
- **Stone/Gem:** Citrine, tiger's eye, gold
- **Geometry:** Octahedron → classical **Air**
- **Pyramid layer:** **Core limestone**, the local Giza bedrock the bulk of the
  pyramid was cut from. *(Grounded.)*

### 4 · GREEN — VERDYN · Heart
- **Light:** green, ~560 THz (the eye's peak sensitivity — the spectrum's hinge)
- **Sound:** 639 Hz ("connection / relationships")
- **Chakra:** Anahata (heart) — love, balance, the center of the seven
- **Stone/Gem:** Emerald, malachite, jade
- **Geometry:** Icosahedron → classical **Water**
- **Pyramid layer:** **Copper & malachite** from Sinai — the metal for tools,
  saws, and chisels, plus its green ore. *(Grounded — Egyptian copper mining in
  Sinai is well attested.)*

### 5 · BLUE — AZULON · Throat
- **Light:** blue, ~640 THz
- **Sound:** 741 Hz ("expression / solving / awakening intuition")
- **Chakra:** Vishuddha (throat) — voice, vibration, truth
- **Stone/Gem:** Sapphire, turquoise, blue lace agate
- **Geometry:** Dodecahedron → classical **Aether / Cosmos**
- **Pyramid layer:** **Tura white limestone** casing (ferried across the Nile
  from Tura) + **Sinai turquoise** (Serabit el-Khadim). *(Grounded — fine Tura
  casing and Sinai turquoise mines are both real.)*

### 6 · INDIGO — INDIGOS · Third Eye
- **Light:** indigo, ~680 THz
- **Sound:** 852 Hz ("returning to spiritual order / intuition")
- **Chakra:** Ajna (third eye) — perception, the pattern behind things
- **Stone/Gem:** Amethyst, lapis lazuli, sodalite
- **Geometry:** **Merkaba** (star tetrahedron) — two interlocked tetrahedra
- **Pyramid layer:** **Dolerite & diorite pounders** from the Eastern Desert —
  the hard hammerstones used to dress the granite. *(Grounded — dolerite pounders
  are a real, well-documented tool.)*

### 7 · VIOLET — VIOLETTE · Crown
- **Light:** violet, ~750 THz (shortest visible wavelength, ~400 nm)
- **Sound:** 963 Hz (the "crown"/"oneness" tone — the spectrum closing to white)
- **Chakra:** Sahasrara (crown) — unity, the capstone
- **Stone/Gem:** Selenite, sugilite, clear quartz
- **Geometry:** **Flower of Life / Sphere** — the seed all the solids unfold from
- **Pyramid layer:** **Egyptian alabaster (calcite)** + the **gold/electrum
  pyramidion** that capped the apex. *(Mixed — alabaster use is real; the gilded
  capstone is widely believed but the original is lost.)*

## Beyond the seven — the hidden tiers (8 · 9 · 0)

The visible spectrum is seven, but the structure doesn't stop there. These three
are **hidden** — present in the code (`THE_HIDDEN` in `data.js`) and whispered on
the victory screen, but kept flexible, "always symbolically researched, measured,
and held toward ultimate truth." They are the room the game grows into.

### 8 · AUREON — Unity (the Eighth)
- **What:** not an eighth creature but **all seven held as one** — the collective
  consciousness, the magnetism in the bone, the prism reassembled into a single
  white light.
- **Color/Light:** white — every ray converged. **Tone:** 792 Hz, the *octave* of
  the 396 root (the spiral returns home, an octave higher).
- **Stone:** clear quartz / diamond / pearl (the stone that holds all stones).
- **Geometry:** the completed Flower of Life; the pyramid whole, capstone rejoined
  to base. **Center:** the Soul-Star (8th chakra, above the crown).
- **Role:** the **polar opposite of the Ninth.** Light's total integration.

### 9 · OUROBOS — The Tail-Eater (the Ninth)
- **What:** the dragon eating its tail. The **dark cycle** — eternal return, the
  shadow that must rise to be seen by the light. *Not evil* — the necessary dark,
  the cocoon, the night before dawn, the turbulence ridden before the resync.
- **Color/Light:** black — no light returned; the swallowing that defines the edge
  of all light. **Tone:** 198 Hz, the *sub-octave* beneath the root — the drone.
- **Stone:** obsidian / onyx / black tourmaline / shungite.
- **Geometry:** the ouroboros ring — torus, lemniscate ∞ laid down, the returning
  spiral. **Center:** the Earth-Star, below the feet.

### 0 · THE NOTHING — The Void / Null
- **What:** **outside the polarity entirely.** Not the Ninth's productive darkness
  but the *absence of story itself* — forgetting, despair, un-creation. It spreads
  where dreaming stops; **selfish wishes feed it by trading away memory** (the
  Neverending tale, esp. the second film: The Nothing; Bastian's wishes cost him
  his memories).
- **Color/Light/Tone:** none — black, silence, the tone that is no tone (0 Hz).
- **The only counter:** a **single grain of imagination** — one name, one
  remembered thing — from which all of it is rebuilt (Fantasia reborn from one
  grain of sand).

```
 0  THE VOID  ── outside all: forgetting, un-creation
 9 ── 8       ── poles: dark cycle (ouroboros)  vs  unity (white light)
[7..1]        ── the visible seven, from one source
```

## How the rungs become a game

- **Type chart (color wheel):** a Hunter resonates (×1.5) with the three colors
  *ahead* of it on the rainbow and is dissonant (×0.75) with the three *behind*.
  A balanced 7-cycle — each beats three, loses to three. See `effectiveness()`.
- **Tone moves:** the signature move plays the creature's solfeggio Hz, so the
  "sound" column isn't decoration — you hear it.
- **The ascent = the pyramid in cross-section:** layer 1 is the granite root,
  layer 7 is the capstone. Climbing the seven battles literally builds the
  pyramid from base to apex.

## Honest footnotes (for the real theory under the fiction)

- **Solfeggio frequencies** (396–963 Hz) come from a 20th-century numerological
  reading of a Latin hymn, not antiquity. They're a lovely organizing device;
  treat them as chosen symbols, not measured physics.
- **Color↔chakra** assignments are the modern (largely 20th-c. Western)
  convention; older Tantric systems differ. Good enough as a consistent key.
- **Light frequencies** are genuine physics (visible light is ~430–750 THz);
  these are the one column that's hard science.
- **Platonic-solid ↔ element** is Plato's *Timaeus*; the fifth solid
  (dodecahedron) he tied to the cosmos/aether.
- **Pyramid stones** are the most defensible historical column — granite,
  basalt, Tura limestone, Sinai copper/turquoise, dolerite pounders, and
  alabaster were all genuinely transported, sometimes hundreds of km. The
  *order* and color-pairing here is the game's invention.
