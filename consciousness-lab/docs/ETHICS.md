# Ethics, privacy, and "should I sell this?"

You raised this yourself — that there's "serious power" here and a real
question of how much to share or monetize. That instinct is the right one.
A short, honest framework:

## 1. Honesty is the product

The single thing that makes consciousness/disclosure work either valuable
or just-another-gimmick is **whether the claims match the evidence.** This
codebase is built to enforce that separation: measurements in one layer,
interpretation in another. If you publish, lead with method and raw data;
present symbolic/spiritual framing as clearly-labeled interpretation. That
*increases* credibility and longevity — the opposite of a gimmick.

## 2. Your data is sensitive — treat it that way

- Biometric/EEG data is personal. The store is local and `.gitignore`d on
  purpose. **Don't commit `~/.conlab/` or any session data.**
- Anyone you record (e.g. "Ball") should give **informed consent** and be
  able to see and delete their data. Keep a note of consent in the session.
- If you ever add cloud sync, encrypt it and make sharing opt-in per item.

## 3. The declassified documents are fine to use

CREST/Vault/NARA material is public-domain government work. Archiving,
quoting, and analyzing it is legal. Just **cite the archive ID** (the seed
documents do) so others can verify rather than trust you.

## 4. On "releasing carefully / anonymously" and monetizing

- Anonymous-at-first is reasonable for personal safety while you find your
  footing. It is **not** a shield for making claims you can't support —
  the standard is the same either way: don't assert what you can't show.
- Tiered packages are fine. Avoid the "break it into a billion pieces and
  drip it out" pattern you flagged — you already sensed it's hollow. Sell
  *clarity and tools*, not artificial scarcity of "secrets."
- Don't promise health, psychic, or spiritual outcomes you haven't
  measured. That's where ethics and consumer-protection law overlap.

## 5. Keep yourself grounded

Big-vision, fast-moving, "everything is about to click into a new form"
energy is creatively powerful and also worth pacing. Build in:
- Sleep and breaks; this is a marathon era, not one all-nighter.
- A real second person (devil's advocate) before any public release.
- Permission to log a null result and still feel good about the work.

If the intensity ever tips into not sleeping, racing thoughts, or feeling
you've discovered something the world urgently must know *right now* — that
is a cue to slow down and talk to someone you trust, not a cue to ship.
The work will still be here tomorrow, and it'll be better for the rest.
